Salix makes digital business cards. To do that, I need some of your details. In this policy you can read which details, why, how long I keep them and what your rights are. I collect as little as possible and use no cookies or trackers.

Who is responsible?

{{site:juridisch.naam}}, {{site:juridisch.adres}}, company number {{site:juridisch.ondernemingsnummer}}. Questions about your data can be sent to {{site:contact.email}}.

Which data, and why?

Free Wallet card

  • Photo of your paper business card. It is read automatically and deleted immediately afterwards. I do not keep the photo.
  • The details on your card (name, job title, company, phone, email, website, address), as you check and confirm them. They appear on your free basic page and in your Wallet card.
  • Your email address, to confirm your address and send you the link to your card.

Legal basis: performing what you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your basic page is not indexed by search engines.

Follow-up emails

Only if you give your consent, I send you at most four emails about your card and the custom options after your free card. Then it stops. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Custom preview, quote and order

  • Your name, job title, contact details, photo and what you tell me about your services: to make your card.
  • For an order: your billing and delivery details and what you ordered.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations such as bookkeeping (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Measuring how the site is used

I count myself which pages and buttons are used and through which link you arrived (e.g. an ad or a referral). I use no cookies for this, and no services such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. A temporary session code stays only in your browser as long as your tab is open. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), namely knowing which parts of the site work.

Who do I share your data with?

I never sell your data. I do work with these service providers, who only process your data on my behalf:

  • Cloudflare: hosting of the site, the cards and the data storage.
  • Anthropic: reading the photo of your paper business card.
  • Stripe: payment. Your card details never reach me.
  • Apple and Google: if you add your card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Some of these services process data outside the European Union. This only happens with appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

How long do I keep your data?

  • Photo of your paper card: not at all, it is deleted after reading.
  • Free Wallet card and basic page: until you delete them yourself via the link in your email.
  • Requests and quotes without an order: 12 months at most.
  • Orders and invoices: as long as the law requires for bookkeeping (10 years).
  • Your custom card: as long as your card is online, and until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict processing, object and take your data with you. You can withdraw consent at any time. To do so, send an email to {{site:contact.email}}. I reply within one month.

Not happy with how I handle your data? You can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

Security

The site and the cards only work over a secure connection (HTTPS), load no third-party scripts and use a strict security policy. The admin area is accessible to me only.

Changes

If this policy changes, you can see it from the date at the top. For important changes, I let card holders know.
